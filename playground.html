Playground

Simulations that run in your browser

These are small simulations and machine learning demos, written in plain JavaScript and sorted into five rooms. You can change the sliders, and in many of them, you can also click or drag on the picture. The ideas behind them are explained on the learn pages, and eight similar simulations also run on an iPhone, in Bead Lab.
